Unsere Geschichte

A little bit Napoli,
a lot of Bavaria.

Germanish started with one question asked too late at a beer festival: what if a Margherita and a Brezel had a baby? Turns out the answer is delicious.

We use slow-fermented Italian dough, San Marzano tomatoes and real fior di latte — then we bring in the good stuff from home: smoky sausages from the butcher, tangy sauerkraut, sharp mustard and mountain cheese.
